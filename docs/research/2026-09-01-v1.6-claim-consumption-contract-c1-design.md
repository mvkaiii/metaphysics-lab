## 1. Research question

Can Python add a deterministic claim-consumption authority between frozen Claim Evidence / Coordination output and Hybrid AI rendering, without changing Phase 3 ranking, claim candidates, domain ordering, confidence authority, or frozen Coordination C semantics?

The new layer answers only whether an already-authorized claim may be consumed by a renderer:

- `render`
- `render_with_caveat`
- `abstain_claim`

It does not create, rerank, rewrite, merge, or delete upstream evidence or claims.

## 2. Architecture position

```text
EvidenceFeature
  -> Phase 3 Ranking
  -> Interpretation v1 authority
  -> Claim Evidence Packet
  -> frozen Coordination C
  -> Claim Consumption Contract C1
  -> Hybrid AI renderer
```

C1 is downstream of Coordination and upstream of Hybrid rendering.

## 3. Frozen upstream authorities

Read-only in this cycle:

- Phase 3 base ranking authority;
- Interpretation v1 semantics/defaults;
- Claim Evidence candidate sets/provenance;
- legacy `cross_system_relation` for audit;
- `coordination_policy_v2.py` C semantics;
- `coordination_relation` as Python authority;
- upstream specificity ceilings.

C1 MUST NOT modify `coordination_policy_v2.py` or reinterpret `coordination_relation`.

## 5. Deterministic decision policy

### 5.1 Validation first

Fail closed when:

- either source bundle digest is invalid;
- target scopes differ;
- ranking digests differ;
- structural interpretation digests differ;
- packet and Coordination domain sets differ;
- duplicate domains exist;
- Coordination relation is unsupported;
- specificity labels are invalid;
- Coordination cap is more permissive than packet authority;
- packet identity/provenance required for consumption is malformed.

Malformed authority is a contract error, not a renderable claim.

### 5.2 Authorized specificity

`authorized_specificity` is the most conservative of packet `effective_specificity` and `coordination_specificity_cap`.

C1 may preserve or downgrade specificity only. It never upgrades.

### 5.3 `abstain_claim`

A valid claim is `abstain_claim` when:

```text
system_support_count == 0
```

Reason: `no_same_scope_target_support`.

The upstream packet remains unchanged for audit.

### 5.4 `render_with_caveat`

A valid claim is `render_with_caveat` when any conservative condition applies:

- `single_system_support`;
- `parallel_context`;
- `parallel_signals`;
- `layered_complement`;
- authorized specificity is lower than packet effective specificity;
- packet confidence is `low_confidence`;
- packet contains an existing abstention limiting event-family, timing, or concrete-event claims.

Canonical reason order:

```text
single_system_support
parallel_context
parallel_signals
layered_complement
specificity_downgraded
low_confidence
existing_abstention
```

### 5.5 `render`

A claim is `render` only when it has at least one same-scope target system and no caveat/abstain condition applies.

## 6. Explicit non-goals

C1 MUST NOT:

- change `primary_domain` or Phase 3 rank;
- create/remove/reorder event-family candidates;
- reorder primary/secondary domains;
- add another display-limit policy;
- alter `EvidenceFeature` or Claim Evidence packets;
- alter `coordination_relation`;
- infer counterevidence absent from upstream schema;
- raise specificity or confidence;
- add probability semantics;
- let AI override Python decisions.

## 7. General/synthetic test matrix

G1. Direct same-domain convergence: `render` when no caveat exists; never raise specificity.

G2. Layered complement: `render_with_caveat`; modifier never becomes a target claim.

G3. Disjoint career + finance / career + partnership: frozen `parallel_signals`; both valid claims remain independently consumable with caveat. Different domains alone never cause abstention/conflict.

G4. Single-system support: `render_with_caveat`; no invented cross-system support.

G5. Malformed/mismatched source authority: fail closed before emitting decisions.

G6. Coordination cap below packet specificity: authorized specificity downgrades.

G7. Coordination cap above packet authority: fail closed; never upgrade.

G8. Determinism and source identity: repeated construction from the exact same valid source bundles yields byte-semantically identical output/digest, and decisions are emitted in canonical `primary_domain` order. Artificially reordered/re-digested upstream bundles are different source identities and are not required to produce the same C1 digest.

G9. Legacy `conflict_or_divergence` never overrides Python `parallel_signals`.

G10. Mutation guard: source bundles, v1 output, Claim Evidence, and Coordination output remain unchanged.

G11. Zero same-scope target support: `abstain_claim` with `no_same_scope_target_support`.
