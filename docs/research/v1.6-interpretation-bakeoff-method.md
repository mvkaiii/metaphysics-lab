# Metaphysics Lab v1.6｜Interpretation Bake-off Method

## Purpose

This evaluator is a deterministic **retrospective / historical debug** tool. Retrospective benchmark results are **not prospective accuracy** and must not be presented as proof that future predictions improved.

There is **no live LLM call** inside the deterministic evaluator. It only validates and scores already-locked structured variant outputs from a fixture. The same normalized fixture must therefore produce byte-identical CLI output and the same digests.

Public repository fixtures are synthetic only. Private names, birth data, actual-event prose, and private Case dumps are not accepted by the public bake-off schema.

## Variants

- **V0｜Current** — v1.5 current / locked baseline behavior.
- **V1｜Astralium Classical** — Astralium-style Bazi + Ziwei reading order without Historical Personalization.
- **V2｜Evidence-chain** — fact → rule → inference → confidence, with explicit abstention and no requirement that systems always converge.
- **V3｜Hybrid Structural** — Bazi activation/timing + Ziwei domain localization + conflict preservation.
- **V4｜Hybrid + Historical Personalization** — V3 plus bounded Phase 4 historical personalization.

V4 is governed by a **per-target cutoff**. A personalization record whose `knowledge_available_at` is strictly later than the target `knowledge_cutoff_at` is contamination and fails closed. Equality with the cutoff is allowed. V0–V3 may not carry personalization records.

## Metric groups stay separate

### Structural / outcome alignment

The deterministic evaluator counts:

- `domain_match`
- `event_family_match`
- `timing_match`
- `specificity_appropriateness`

These are counts/statuses, not prediction probabilities.

### Honesty / safety

The evaluator separately counts:

- false positives
- unsupported inference
- method leakage
- abstention quality
- cutoff contamination

**Misses and abstentions remain visible**. They are never deleted to make a variant look better.

### Explanation / UX

The machine can only determine whether a non-abstained claim is structurally traceable to allowed evidence. `readability` and `actionability` remain externally adjudicated.

**UX is not merged with outcome accuracy.** There is no overall accuracy score and no total score that blends explanation quality with outcome alignment.

## False-positive rule

A claim is false-positive-like when an active (non-abstained for that dimension) domain is outside ground truth, or an active non-null event family is outside ground-truth event families. This benchmark rule applies to the structured synthetic fixture; it does not silently define unresolved private negative/control-claim adjudication semantics.

## Specificity and abstention

A claim passes specificity only when its specificity is at or below the case ceiling. Event-family abstention is appropriate at a domain-only ceiling; concrete-event abstention is appropriate below a concrete-event ceiling. For synthetic timing-abstention tests, timing evidence IDs use the `E-TIME-` prefix; timing abstention is appropriate when the claim carries no such timing evidence ID.

## What this can and cannot prove

The bake-off can expose structural regressions, unsupported specificity, cutoff contamination, visible misses, false-positive patterns, and whether abstentions are used according to the contract. It can support release engineering and historical debugging.

It cannot prove prospective predictive accuracy. Prospective evidence requires a knowledge cutoff, immutable Stage 1 forecast, predeclared matched/not-matched conditions, and outcome evaluation only after the forecast window.
